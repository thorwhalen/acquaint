acquaint.digests#

Digests: what a conversation with an entity said, written up through a watermark.

A digest is one Markdown file per conversation, in the entity’s folder (people/<id>/digests/<name>.md, and the same under projects, orgs and groups). Its frontmatter is the state of the processing, and its body is the notes:

schema: acquaint/digest/v1
ref: whatsapp:ada-lovelace        # the correspond conversation reference: the SSOT
archive: correspond               # where the raw messages live (never this store)
through:                          # the watermark, per scope: processed up to this time
  content: '2026-11-03T19:37:47+01:00'
  style: '2026-06-21T19:27:45+02:00'
updated: '2026-11-04'

through answers “from which date and time is this conversation new?”: hand it to correspond read <ref> --since <through> to get what has not been processed. Two scopes exist because a conversation can be mined for how someone writes (style, the acquaint-profile skill) separately from what it said (content, the acquaint-digest skill).

The body has five sections: Orientation (who is in it, what it is for, its span), Takeaways (decisions, agreements, asks, facts the operator will need), Themes (the recurring subjects, each with where it comes up), Index (the conversation cut into dated sections so an agent can go straight to one) and Open threads (what is waiting on whom). Every item in those sections carries an anchor, [at: <ISO time>] (a message time in the archive: rg -n '^2026-07-03T14:02' <transcript>) or a [source: …]; the messages themselves stay in the archive, never here (POLICY.md: no whole message bodies).

>>> digest_name("whatsapp:ada-lovelace"), digest_name("github:example/demo#12")
('digests/whatsapp-ada-lovelace.md', 'digests/github-example-demo-12.md')
